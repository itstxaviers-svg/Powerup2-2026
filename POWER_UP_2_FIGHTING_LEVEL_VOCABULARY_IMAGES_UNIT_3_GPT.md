Reasons:

- `dentist` and `nurse` duplicate the concepts of `a dentist` and `a nurse`; producing both would create indistinguishable picture questions.
- Appearance adjectives such as `fair`, `fat`, `short`, `tall` and `thin` are ambiguous without comparison and may encourage unhelpful or insensitive character depiction.

- Abstract words and actions such as `only`, `as a`, `good at`, `meet`, `invite` and `come in` do not have one unique text-free visual answer.
- Location and party images can introduce many secondary objects, visual clutter and overlap with profession or action clues.

Do not substitute additional images for these excluded targets. This batch must remain exactly 13 files.
